# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically rigorous community. Be constructive, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, protect private information, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to [smartbytecoder@gmail.com](mailto:smartbytecoder@gmail.com). Do not include credentials, customer data, or unrelated sensitive information. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
